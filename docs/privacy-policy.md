# Cravage privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-15*

Cravage is designed so that there is very little to collect, and this page says exactly what that
little is.

## Your private figure

The figure you type is processed on your phone. What the app sends to the other participants is a
masked version of it: your figure plus random amounts that cancel out when everyone's masked
numbers are added together. No phone, including the host's, can recover your figure from what it
receives, unless every other participant in the round conspires against you. This relies on
everyone checking the room code and counting the phones in the room. Otherwise a host could add
pretend participants that exist only on their own phone, and those would count as conspirators.
Your figure is held in memory only while the round is running and is cleared when it ends.

## What Cravage sends, and to whom

During a round your phone sends, to the other phones in the room: your nickname, your masked
number, digital signatures that let the others check nothing was tampered with, a random number
used to make the room code, and a confirmation that you saw the same room code (or, if you tap
"The codes don't match", a signed note saying so). Phones connect to each other directly over Wi-Fi. Cravage does not
operate a server that receives your round data.

## What nearby phones can see

While a room is open, nearby phones running Cravage can see the room's label (what is being
averaged), the host's nickname and the group size, and so can any other nearby device, or any
device on the same Wi-Fi network, that looks for them. Shortly after the round starts, the room's
listing drops the label and nickname and shows only a made-up name, until the host leaves. Once you
join, the other participants see your nickname.

## What is saved on your phone

Cravage saves your nickname and your light or dark appearance choice. It does not save round
history, and figures are never written to storage. Whether you have bought the one-off unlock is
kept by Apple's App Store, not by Cravage.

## Exported transcripts

You can choose to share a transcript of a round that every phone agreed on. It contains the room's
label, the masked numbers, signatures and result, never anyone's figure or nickname. Cravage writes
the file only when you pick where to send it, and deletes its own copy when you leave the result
screen. Once sent, whoever receives it can keep it and share it.

## Purchases

The optional one-off unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your
payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

## Local network permission

iOS asks for permission to find devices on the local network. Cravage uses it only to find other
phones nearby running Cravage.

## Analytics and diagnostics

Cravage has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service. If you ask for help, you can copy
a short diagnostic summary from Settings, or tap Report a problem, which opens an email to the
address below with the summary filled in. The summary contains the app and iOS versions, the phone
model, the protocol stage, counts (such as how many phones confirmed the room code or how many sends
failed), timings in seconds (such as how long a connection had been quiet), the kind of
connection each link used (such as peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or a Wi-Fi network, never a network's name)
and error codes, including for the last round you left. It never contains a figure, a nickname, a
room name, a key or the room code. It is only sent if you send it.

## Children

Cravage does not knowingly collect any information from children. Apart from an email you choose to
send to support, it collects no information from anyone.
