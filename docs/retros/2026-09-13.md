# Retro 2026-09-13 - planning session

Public-safe record. Scope: the grilling interview, plan approval, independent review, revision 2,
and repository bring-up (the initial commit).

## Went well

- Four interview rounds settled the product, money, process and working-setup decisions with no
  re-litigation; the decision log lived in the plan from round one, so revision 2 was an edit.
- Fact-finding ran ahead of the questions: the 8-device session cap, hosting prices and the
  marketing tooling's existence were known before anything depended on them.
- The independent plan review was verified claim by claim against Apple's own pages before acting;
  all held and were folded in the same day.
- CI green on the first day; the reference verifier pinned by commit and checksum.

## Went badly

- Two platform facts were stated as settled and were wrong (Multipeer Connectivity as a long-term
  foundation; the device name as a default nickname). Root cause: secondary sources passed on as
  facts. Caught by the independent reviewer.
- The finite-mask leakage with unbounded inputs was not caught in planning. Root cause: the web
  app's pinned test vectors were treated as the specification instead of deriving requirements
  from the privacy claim.
- CI went red twice for Windows artefacts: a lost execute bit, then a checksum of a CRLF working
  copy. Root cause: a Mac-targeted repo built from Windows before any
  line-ending gate existed.

## Gates

- Platform facts: every load-bearing platform claim in a plan cites a vendor page fetched
  in-session or is labelled unverified. Check: the plan's facts table carries a source per row; the
  council checklist item 6 enforces it in review. No automated truth check exists; this is the
  named fallback.
- Windows-to-Mac artefacts: `.gitattributes` pins LF, checksums are of stored bytes, CI is the
  check. Closed.
